## **📊 Key Insights**

• Employee Overview: The organization has 1,470 employees, with 237 employees leaving, resulting in an attrition rate of 16.1%.

• Department Analysis: The Sales department records the highest attrition, indicating a need for improved employee engagement and retention initiatives.

• Job Role Analysis: Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives experience the highest employee turnover, suggesting these roles require focused retention strategies.

• Age Group Analysis: Employees aged 26–35 years show the highest attrition, highlighting that early and mid-career employees are more likely to leave the organization.

• Business Recommendation: Strengthening career development opportunities, improving employee satisfaction, and implementing targeted retention programs in high-attrition departments and job roles can help reduce employee turnover.
